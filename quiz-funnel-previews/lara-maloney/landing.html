Who this is for

This scorecard is for you if:

  • You and your partner earn $150k+ between you
  • You own a home, with a mortgage that's still doing most of the work
  • You're somewhere in your 30s or 40s — the years where every decision compounds
  • You've thought about getting financial advice, but haven't pulled the trigger

It's not for you if:

  • You're after stock tips or quick returns — that's not what we do
  • You want someone to manage your KiwiSaver and call it a day
  • You're already working with an adviser you trust — keep them

Three things people ask before they start

Is this a sales pitch dressed up as a scorecard?

No. About one in four people who finish the scorecard are a fit for what we do. The other three get a useful result, a planning guide if they want it, and never hear from us again. We'd rather tell you it's not the right time than waste your hour.

What happens with my email?

It goes into our system so we can send you your full result and the Wealth Planning Guide. You can unsubscribe in one click. We don't sell, share, or trade it — not how we operate.

How accurate can it be in 3 minutes?

The score is a directional read, not a financial plan. It tells you which of the three bands you're in and the three gaps most worth closing. If you want the real plan, that's what the Discovery session is for — and the scorecard tells you whether one's worth booking.
