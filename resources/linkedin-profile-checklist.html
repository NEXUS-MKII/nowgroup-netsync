LinkedIn Profile Checklist

The 8 foundations every LinkedIn profile needs before anyone sees it. Two halves — turn off notifications, work the foundations, then write the story.

Part 1 of 2 Items 1–4
LinkedIn profile checklist — foundations
Complete these before anyone sees your profile
4 of 8 fundamentals
1
Turn off profile update notifications
Before making any changes, stop your network being alerted to every edit.
Settings & Privacy → Sharing Profile Edits → toggle to No
2
Profile photo
High-resolution professional headshot. Friendly and approachable.
14x more likely to be viewed with a photo
400 × 400px recommended
3
Personal cover photo
A professional image that reflects your brand — not a generic or personal photo.
1584 × 396px · JPG, PNG or GIF · under 8MB
4
Compelling headline
Describes your expertise and value — not just your job title. Appears in every search result.
Up to 120 characters · lead with keywords · what you do for whom

Part 2 of 2 Items 5–8
LinkedIn profile checklist — your story
What people read once your foundation brings them to your profile
8 of 8 fundamentals
5
Custom public profile URL
Cleaner, shareable, and looks professional in email signatures and bios.
linkedin.com/in/yourname · add middle initial if your name is common
6
Contact information
Email, phone, and any relevant links. Make it easy for the right people to reach you.
Check it's current — stale contact info loses warm connections
7
About section (summary)
Conversational, keyword-rich, and organised into short paragraphs. First or third person — pick one and stick to it.
Under 2,000 characters
Short paragraphs only · no formal titles · include keywords for SEO
8
Media and featured content
A few relevant documents, links, videos or presentations. Makes your profile tangible — not just text.
Quality over quantity
Don't overload · pin your best work to Featured
