The VA Readiness Framework: 5 Stages to Offshore Success

Diagnostic tool for service business owners considering VA partnerships
01
Stage 1
Stage 1: Not Ready — Your processes exist only in your head. ☐ You handle all client communication personally ☐ No documented procedures for common tasks ☐ Revenue depends entirely on your daily involvement
02
Stage 2
Stage 2: Getting Organised — Basic systems emerging but still owner-dependent. ☐ Some email templates and processes written down ☐ Calendar management could be delegated ☐ Clear qualification criteria for prospects
03
Stage 3
Stage 3: Delegation Ready — Systems documented, ready for handover. ☐ Written procedures for lead response and follow-up ☐ Template library for common scenarios ☐ Comfortable with someone else handling initial contact
04
Stage 4
Stage 4: VA Integrated — Offshore team member handling operations smoothly. ☐ VA manages lead response within 2-hour target ☐ Pre-qualification process running automatically ☐ Calendar and follow-up systems fully delegated
05
Stage 5
Stage 5: Revenue Partner — VA contributing directly to sales outcomes. ☐ VA conducts initial sales conversations ☐ Lead-to-call conversion rates consistently above 40% ☐ VA identified and nurtures high-value prospects
