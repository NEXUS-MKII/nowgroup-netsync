The 5-Stage VA Partnership Readiness Framework

Know exactly where you are and what comes next
01
Stage 1
Stage 1: Lead Overflow — You have more leads than you can handle personally ✓ Missing calls because you're on jobs ✓ Leads waiting 24+ hours for callbacks ✓ Considering turning off marketing when busy
02
Stage 2
Stage 2: System Ready — You have lead tracking in place ✓ Standard pricing and service packages ✓ Appointment booking system that others can use ✓ Basic scripts for common inquiries
03
Stage 3
Stage 3: Process Documented — Written procedures for lead qualification ✓ Clear handover process from VA to you ✓ Quality standards and call monitoring ✓ Backup systems when VA is unavailable
04
Stage 4
Stage 4: Partnership Active — VA handles 80%+ of lead calls ✓ Conversion rates maintained or improved ✓ Client feedback positive about phone experience ✓ Monthly performance reviews happening
05
Stage 5
Stage 5: Scale Mode — Marketing budget increased confidently ✓ VA role expanded beyond just calls ✓ Long-term partnership (6+ months) ✓ Considering second VA for growth
