The Spine — what holds this together
  • SECTION 1 OF 4 — PARTNER READINESS ASSESSMENT
  • ☐ You regularly discuss IT challenges with SME clients (5-25 staff)
  • ☐ Your clients ask you about reliable IT support options
  • ☐ You've seen clients struggle with unexpected IT costs or downtime
  • ☐ You understand the difference between reactive and managed IT services
  • ☐ Your clients trust your recommendations on business service providers

The Shift — frameworks that reposition the problem

  • SECTION 2 OF 4 — CLIENT IT PAIN DIAGNOSTIC FLOW
  • REACTIVE IT TRAP → Client calls IT contractor only when things break
  • COST SPIRAL → Bills spike every quarter with no predictable budget
  • SECURITY BLIND SPOTS → No proactive monitoring or threat management
  • CAPABILITY GAP → Internal IT person can't cover every domain
Red flag
🚩 RED FLAG: If you're referring SME clients to IT contractors for ongoing support instead of managed service providers, you're missing the partnership opportunity that delivers predictable outcomes for growing businesses.
