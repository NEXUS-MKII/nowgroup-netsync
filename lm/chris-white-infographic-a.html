The Volume Trap Diagnostic — Is Your Networking Broken?

NOW GROUP
Chris White
Section 1

Activity Measurement Red Flags

  • Counting events attended over partnerships built
  • Measuring LinkedIn connections over meaningful conversations
  • Tracking business cards over follow-up quality
  • Celebrating busy calendars over revenue outcomes
  • Focusing on quantity over relationship depth
Section 2

Spreading Thin Warning Signs

  • No clear target partner profiles
  • Attending every networking event available
  • Generic value propositions for all audiences
  • No systematic approach to relationship building
  • Inconsistent follow-up across all connections
Section 3

Goodwill Burning Indicators

  • Random, sporadic contact with partners
  • Generic 'checking in' messages
  • No structured value delivery system
  • Partnerships fade within 3-6 months
  • Referrals become increasingly rare
Section 4

This Week's Action Steps

  • Identify your top 3 target partner types
  • Audit last month's networking activities for outcomes
  • Create systematic follow-up framework
  • Schedule partnership review sessions
  • Design specific value delivery plan
Red flag
RED FLAG: If you can't name 3 active partnerships generating monthly referrals, you're in the Volume Trap
